THE DEMO

BEAT 0 — BEFORE THE SCREEN (2 min)

Do NOT open the laptop yet. Stand or sit facing the officers. Make eye contact.

SAY:

"Thank you for making the time. Before I show you anything —

Grosint is already being used by police officers across multiple states in India. Our OSINT tool is deployed in the field.

Anveshak is our AI intelligence platform. Built under iDEX ADITI for defence, now adapted for law enforcement. What I'm about to show you is not a presentation — it's a live system monitoring cyber fraud activity in Telangana right now.

We loaded Telegram channels where mule account operators recruit — GamingPay, DoingPay, AlphaPay — along with your local news sources and even darkweb forums. Let me show you what the system found on its own."

WHY THIS WORKS:

  • "Police across India" — peer validation [L3-Status: relative position]
  • "iDEX ADITI" — government-vetted, not random startup [L2-Signaling: costly signal]
  • Naming specific Telegram channels — shows you did homework, not generic pitch
  • "Let me show what it found on its own" — curiosity, not feature list

PAUSE. Let them react. If they nod or say "show me" — open the laptop.

BEAT 1 — LOGIN (30 sec)

  1. Open http://localhost:3000
  2. Point at the login screen briefly:
    • "For Official Use Only" classification bar
    • अन्वेषक — "the seeker" in Devanagari
  3. Login: demo_cyber@anveshak.local
  4. Land on Topics Dashboard

SAY:

"This is the analyst workbench. One topic per mission. Right now we have one running — Telangana Cyber Fraud Intelligence. Your cyber cell could have five running simultaneously — investment fraud, sextortion, digital arrest scams, loan app fraud, each independent."

DO NOT linger on the dashboard. Click into the topic immediately.

BEAT 2 — THE CONTENT FEED (2 min)

  1. Click "Telangana Cyber Fraud Intelligence" → Content Feed opens
  2. Scroll slowly. Let them SEE the volume:

SAY (while scrolling):

"Over 700 items collected. 22 sources across 5 platforms — Telegram channels, news RSS feeds, TGCSB official website, and darkweb forums. The system has been running for a few days — this is what it crawled automatically."
  1. Point at specific cards as you scroll:
    • A Telegram message from GamingPay191 or IndiaBankAccs
    • An RSS item from Telangana Today or Siasat Daily
    • A darkweb item (if visible)
"See the platform badges — Telegram, RSS, Web, Darkweb. Every item has a timestamp showing when it was captured. This runs 24/7 without any officer touching it."
  1. Point at a credibility score on a card:
"Every item carries the credibility of its source. Telangana Today scores higher than an anonymous Telegram channel. The analyst sees this at a glance — no guesswork."

DO NOT explain how scraping works. DO NOT mention Crawl4AI, embeddings, or NLP.

BEAT 3 — CLUSTER VIEW — THE CORE DEMO (2.5 min)

  1. Switch to Cluster View

SAY:

"Now this is where it gets interesting. The AI reads every article, every Telegram message, and groups them by narrative — which stories are related."
  1. Point out clusters. Walk through them top to bottom:

Cluster: "TGCSB Cyber Fraud" (35 items, 3 sources)

"35 items from 3 independent sources — news reports about TGCSB operations, cyber awareness campaigns, and fraud case updates. The system grouped them because they're all about TGCSB's response to cyber crime."

Cluster: "Telangana Cyber Security Bureau" (11 items)

"TGCSB official communications and press releases. The system separated these from general news because the tone and source are different — official versus journalistic."

THIS IS YOUR TRANSITION LINE:

"Nobody searched for any of this. Nobody typed a keyword. The system read 700+ items, grouped them into narratives, and told you: here are the stories forming around cyber fraud in your state right now."

PAUSE. Let that land. 3 seconds of silence.

BEAT 4 — SIGNALS (1.5 min)

  1. Click Signals in sidebar
  2. Show the badge count (14 signals)

SAY:

"When multiple independent sources report the same narrative, the system fires a signal. 14 signals generated automatically."
  1. Point at a specific signal:
  • "TGCSB — Hyderabad — Telangana confirmed by 3 independent sources"
"Three independent sources — news, official website, Telegram — all talking about the same fraud pattern. This isn't one person complaining. This is a verified pattern. The system tells your analyst: pay attention."
  1. Point at another:
  • "Sentiment dropped 0.30 in last 24h"
"Sentiment shift — the tone around cyber fraud in Telangana turned sharply negative. Could be a major fraud case breaking, public anger rising. The system detected the shift and flagged it."
  1. Acknowledge one signal (click). Dismiss another (click).
"Every signal is triaged by the analyst. Acknowledged, dismissed, or escalated. Full audit trail — who saw it, when, what action."

DO NOT explain ISC (independent source count) in technical terms. Say "independent sources" — TGCSB understands that language.

BEAT 5 — IDENTIFIER INTELLIGENCE (2 min) — THE KILLER FEATURE

  1. Navigate to Identifier Clusters (if available in workspace tab)

SAY:

"The system doesn't just read text. It extracts phone numbers, Telegram handles, UPI IDs, and links them across sources."
  1. Show the DoingPay network:
  • doingpay_ff, doingpay_sh, doingpay_vv, doingpay_cc — all linked
"Look at this — four Telegram handles, all variations of DoingPay. The system found them across different messages and linked them automatically. This is a payment fraud network. Same operators running multiple channels with slightly different names."
  1. Show qqpays.online domain:
"qqpays.online — a suspicious payment domain found in Telegram messages. No legitimate payment processor uses this. This is the kind of indicator your cyber cell can act on immediately."
  1. Show the Hong Kong phone numbers (+852):
"And here's the cross-border angle. Hong Kong phone numbers appearing in Indian fraud channels. These aren't Indian operators — this is international. The system extracted these automatically from Telegram messages."
  1. Frame the capability, not the specific result:
"Now imagine this with the Telegram channels your cyber cell actually monitors. A phone number appears in a fraud complaint on one channel, then in a mule recruitment post on another, then linked to a UPI ID on a third. The system connects those dots. No officer searched for it — the machine linked it."

THIS IS THE RETELLABLE MOMENT.

If the officer remembers one thing, it should be: "The system finds fraud networks across Telegram channels automatically."

DO NOT say this is dummy data. Everything you showed is REAL scraped data. The Telegram handles are real. The mechanism is real. Frame the fraud network use case as what happens when THEIR sources are added.

BEAT 6 — SOURCE CREDIBILITY (1 min)

  1. Click Sources in sidebar
  2. Show the list — credibility bars, health indicators

SAY:

"Every source has a credibility score. Telangana Today: 80. An anonymous Telegram channel: 10. The system auto-adjusts — if a source's reports are confirmed by other independent sources, score goes up. If it publishes noise, score drops."
  1. Click on one source → show audit log tab
"Every credibility change is logged. Timestamp, old score, new score, reason. Immutable. Nobody can silently change a source's trustworthiness. When a report cites this source, the credibility at that exact moment is frozen into the report."

DO NOT demo a credibility update manually. Just show the audit trail exists.

BEAT 7 — REPORT (1.5 min)

  1. Click Reports in sidebar
  2. Show the existing report (or generate live if confident in timing)

SAY:

"This is the output. An AI-generated intelligence brief."
  1. Show:
    • Markdown-rendered report with source citations
    • Source inventory table (22 sources across 5 platforms)
    • Identified indicators — DoingPay handles, qqpays.online, phone numbers
    • Confidence score (80%)
"Every claim cites a source. Every source has a credibility score. The report is generated by an AI running on THIS machine — qwen2, a 7-billion parameter model running locally via Ollama. No data leaves this deployment. No cloud API. Fully sovereign."

SAY (if report was generated live):

"That took about 4 minutes. An analyst writing this manually? An hour?"

BEAT 8 — THE CLOSE (1.5 min, no clicks)

Close the laptop lid halfway or lean back. Signal: demo is over, conversation begins.

SAY:

Cluster — AI grouped narratives: fraud operations, TGCSB response, mule recruitment patterns

All on one machine. No cloud. No data leaves your network.

We can set up a 30-day pilot on your existing hardware. Your sources, your topics, your analysts using it daily. No procurement paperwork for a pilot. If it doesn't produce actionable leads in 30 days, we pack up."

THEN SHUT UP.

Let them speak. Whatever they say next tells you exactly where they are.

IF LIVE DEMO FAILS

Technology fails. Have a plan:

  1. Internet down / containers crashed: Use screenshots (take them now)
  2. Report generation hangs: Show the existing report, say "let me show one we generated earlier — same content"
  3. Page loads slowly: Fill the silence: "The system is running 22 sources in real-time on this machine — processing power is shared"
  4. Login fails: Have the password written down separately. Don't type it wrong in front of officers.
  5. Ollama OOM: Stop heavy containers before demo (vision, social worker, alertmanager). Ollama needs 5GB.
