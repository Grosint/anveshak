What Anveshak Does
🌐
Open Web & News Monitoring
Crawls news sites, forums, paste sites, and RSS feeds. Detects emerging narratives and clusters related articles automatically. Multilingual — Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, Bengali, 200+ languages.

Social Media & Messaging Intelligence
Native adapters for Telegram, WhatsApp, Reddit, X/Twitter, Bluesky, Instagram, YouTube. Monitors channels, groups, and hashtags in real-time. Extracts phone numbers, UPI IDs, crypto wallets, and bank accounts automatically.
