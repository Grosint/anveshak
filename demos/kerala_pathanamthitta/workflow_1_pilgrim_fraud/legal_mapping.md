# Workflow 1: Pilgrim Cyber Fraud — Evidence-to-Legal Section Mapping

## Scenario Summary

Fake Sabarimala accommodation booking website "darshanmokshahomestay.in" targeting
pilgrims during Mandalam/Makaravilakku season. Telegram group amplification in
Malayalam and Hindi. UPI mule accounts spanning Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and
Haryana. ~80 victims, Rs 65 lakh aggregate loss (all figures synthetic).
